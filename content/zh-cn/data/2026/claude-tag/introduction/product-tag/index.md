@Claude 在工作发生的地方实时响应。即日起面向 Slack 中的 Claude Enterprise 与 Team 开放。你把它 Tag 进来，它会把你 Tag 回去。

@Claude reads threads, understands full context, and reacts in real time so your team moves forward together. Bring Claude into your channel.

@Claude 会阅读线程、理解完整上下文，并实时响应，让团队一起往前走。把 Claude 请进你的频道。

Available in beta for Claude Enterprise and Team customers in Slack.

目前以 beta 提供给 Slack 中的 Claude Enterprise 与 Team 客户。

Tag @Claude in Teams, where it will read the conversation, understand context, and respond in real time.

在 Teams 里 Tag @Claude，它会阅读对话、理解上下文，并实时响应。

## How it works / 它如何工作

Tag Claude into any thread in Slack, no matter how long or messy. It reacts to context, decisions, or open questions to help busy teams get more done.

把 Claude Tag 进 Slack 里任意线程，无论多长、多乱。它会对上下文、决策或未决问题作出反应，帮助忙碌的团队多做成事。

**Tag it in.** @Claude in any thread or channel and it picks up the context everyone already shares. Ask it to run the numbers, file a ticket, or organize a chaotic chat into action items. It reads the room and does the work.

**把它 Tag 进来。** 在任意线程或频道里 @Claude，它会接上大家已经共享的上下文。让它跑数字、建工单，或把一团乱的聊天整理成待办。它读懂现场，然后干活。

**Set a schedule or let it run long tasks.** Give @Claude standing instructions and it works without a mention now or in the future. Ask it to watch a channel, run a weekly digest, flag anything urgent, or page the right person.

**设定日程，或让它跑长任务。** 给 @Claude 常驻指令，它现在或将来都不必再被提及也能工作。让它盯一个频道、做每周摘要、标出紧急事项，或呼叫对该的人。

**It jumps in, and can tag you back.** @Claude doesn't wait to be asked. It surfaces the thread that went quiet, posts when the deploy is live, and flags the decision that needs your call.

**它会主动介入，也可以把你 Tag 回去。** @Claude 不会干等被叫。它会把冷掉的线程翻上来，在部署上线时发帖，并标出需要你拍板的决策。

## How teams use @Claude / 团队如何使用 @Claude

Claude in Slack can share work in the format your team needs, right in the thread.

Slack 里的 Claude 能以团队需要的形式，直接在线程里交出工作成果。

**Catch up fast.** Prompt: `@Claude What got decided here and what's still open?`  
Get everything you need from a long thread in seconds. @Claude can surface decisions, open questions, and stakeholders, and you see exactly what's waiting on you.

**快速赶上进度。** 提示词：`@Claude What got decided here and what's still open?`  
几秒内从长线程里拿到你需要的一切。@Claude 能标出决策、未决问题和相关干系人，你也能看清哪些正等着你。

**Pull the numbers.** Prompt: `@Claude Top 20 enterprise accounts by spend, last 7 and 28 days.`  
Ask @Claude to query your data and it posts results like metrics, benchmarks, and charts in the channel, so the whole team sees the answer and can keep asking questions right there.

**把数字拉出来。** 提示词：`@Claude Top 20 enterprise accounts by spend, last 7 and 28 days.`  
让 @Claude 查询数据，它会把指标、基准和图表发到频道里，全团队都能看见答案，并就地继续追问。

**Build from the thread.** Prompt: `@Claude Fix the bug in this thread and open a draft PR.`  
Turn a bug report into a draft PR from the context in the thread without ever leaving Slack. Get more done without switching tabs.

**从线程里直接做出来。** 提示词：`@Claude Fix the bug in this thread and open a draft PR.`  
利用线程里已有的上下文，把缺陷报告变成草稿 PR，而不必离开 Slack。少切标签页，多做成事。

**Prep before calls.** Prompt: `@Claude I'm meeting Acme at 2 — what do I need to know?`  
Walk into every meeting with a full briefing. CRM notes, recent threads, and call history pulled together before you think to ask.

**会前准备。** 提示词：`@Claude I'm meeting Acme at 2 — what do I need to know?`  
带着完整简报走进每一次会议。CRM 笔记、最近线程和通话记录，在你想到要问之前就已经汇总好。

**Watch what matters.** Prompt: `@Claude Watch this channel, triage what comes in, and work through the backlog. Tag me only when something needs my call.`  
@Claude can manage work in the channels your team never had bandwidth for: monitoring backlogs, triaging alerts, and surfacing what needs a human to review, so work that keeps getting pushed out can finally be completed.

**盯住真正重要的事。** 提示词：`@Claude Watch this channel, triage what comes in, and work through the backlog. Tag me only when something needs my call.`  
@Claude 能管理团队一直腾不出手的频道：监控积压、分诊告警、把需要人审的事项翻上来，那些总被往后推的工作终于能做完。

## Core capabilities / 核心能力

**Understands your org.** The longer Claude works with your team, the more it understands how your organization thinks, how decisions get made, and who owns what.

**理解你的组织。** Claude 和团队共事越久，就越理解组织如何思考、决策如何作出、以及谁负责什么。

**Memory across threads and days.** Context can draw on previous conversations and memory. What happened in Monday's standup is still there on Thursday, without anyone repeating it.

**跨线程、跨天的记忆。** 上下文可以调用先前的对话和记忆。周一站会里发生的事，周四还在，不必有人再讲一遍。

**A shared resource in your channel.** Claude surfaces what matters to the whole channel, not just the person who asked. Everyone sees the work, and anyone can build on it.

**频道里的共享资源。** Claude 把对整个频道重要的事翻上来，而不只给提问的那个人。所有人都看见工作，任何人都可以在上面继续做。

**Proactive action instead of asking.** Set a standing instruction once. Claude watches the channel, runs routines, flags what's urgent, and tags you back when something needs a decision.

**主动行动，而不是等人问。** 常驻指令设一次即可。Claude 盯着频道、跑 routines、标出紧急事项，并在需要决策时把你 Tag 回去。

**In the work and tools with you.** Shape how Claude works in each channel with skills and instructions. Give it access to tools and data to take action on your behalf and post results.

**和你一起待在工作与工具里。** 用 skills 和指令塑造 Claude 在每个频道里的工作方式。给它工具和数据的访问权，让它代为行动并把结果发出来。

**Runs tasks that take days.** Hand Claude a complex task and it works while you move on to other projects. It follows up, asks for input, or comes back when it's done.

**能跑耗时数天的任务。** 把复杂任务交给 Claude，你转去做别的项目时它继续干。它会跟进、征求意见，或在做完后回来。

## @Claude connects to your tools / @Claude 连接到你的工具

Configure any tool with an API using Agent Identity. Claude can query, act, and report back in the thread where the work is happening.

用 Agent Identity 配置任何带 API 的工具。Claude 可以查询、行动，并在工作发生的那条线程里回报。

延伸阅读： [Agent identity](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/agent-identity)

## Admins stay in control / 管理员保持掌控

Claude acts under its own identity in your systems. Admins configure access once; everyone else just types @Claude.

Claude 在你的系统里以自己的身份行动。管理员配置一次访问权限；其他人只需输入 @Claude。

**Customizable identities for granular governance.** Claude has its own account in your systems, not a borrowed login. Every credential’s use is logged, so "what did Claude do, and who asked for it?" always has an answer.

**可定制身份，用于细粒度治理。** Claude 在你的系统里有自己的账号，而不是借用某个人的登录。每一次凭证使用都会被记录，因此「Claude 做了什么、是谁让它做的」总有答案。

**Add it per channel.** Decide which tools Claude can reach and read. Confine sensitive connectors to a single private channel or enable broader access for specific tools and resources. Adjust any time from the admin console.

**按频道添加。** 决定 Claude 能触及和阅读哪些工具。把敏感连接限制在单个私有频道，或为特定工具与资源开放更广的访问。可随时在管理控制台调整。
